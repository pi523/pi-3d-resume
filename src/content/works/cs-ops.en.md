A company with well over a decade of customer-operations experience had its playbook living in people's heads and process docs. The job: translate that playbook into AI workflows that run in production — built together with the client team, one iteration at a time.

I worked forward-deployed: first scoping the requirements with the client into five workflows (customer profiling, intent detection, product recommendations, after-sales support, human escalation) and prototyping them for the team to try; then wiring in WeChat, CRM, orders, logistics, coupons and internal data systems so the agents reasoned over real context rather than isolated chats.

After handover to the client's customer-operations team, average response time dropped by about 60%. What stayed with me wasn't the tech but the co-delivery itself: for an agent to reach production, whoever designs it has to sit inside the business first.
